# Telecom-Churn-Analysis-PowerBI_
Telecom Customer Churn Analysis dashboard built using Power BI to analyze customer churn, tenure, contracts, services, customer segments, and revenue through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

📉 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

A 3-page Power BI dashboard that analyzes churn for 7,043 telecom customers. It shows how many customers are leaving, which segments churn the most, and how revenue, tenure and services relate to churn.

🎯 Objectives
Measure the overall churn rate
Find the customer segments with the highest churn
Understand revenue by contract type and internet service
Check how tenure and add-on services affect churn

📊 Dashboard Pages
1️⃣ Churn Overview
KPIs: Total Customers (7,043), Churned Customers (1,869), Churn Rate (26.54%), Average Tenure (32.37 months)
Visuals: Churned customers by contract, payment method and internet service, plus a churn vs non-churn donut
Slicers: Gender, Payment Method, Contract, Tenure Group

2️⃣ Customer & Revenue Analysis
KPIs: Average Monthly Charges (64.76), Total Revenue (16.06M), Average Revenue per Customer (2.28K), Senior Citizen Customers (1,142)
Visuals: Revenue by contract and internet service, average monthly charges by internet service, customers by payment method and tech support

3️⃣ Service & Churn Analysis
KPIs: Churned Month-to-Month Customers (1,655), Churn Rate with No Tech Support (41.64%), Fiber Optic Churn Rate (41.89%), Churned Customers with Online Security (295)
Visuals: Churn rate by tenure group, and customers by online security, device protection and internet service with churn

🔍 Key Insights
26.54% of customers churned, about 1 in 4.
Month-to-month contracts account for 1,655 of the 1,869 churned customers (~88.5%).
Electronic check users churn the most (1,071), far ahead of mailed check (308), bank transfer (258) and credit card (232).
Fiber optic has the highest churn rate (41.89%) with 1,297 churned customers, even though it also earns the most revenue (9.9M).
New customers leave first: churn is 47% in the first 12 months and drops to 7% after 61–72 months.
Add-on services help retention: customers without Device Protection churn at 39.13% vs 22.50% with it, and customers without Tech Support churn at 41.64%.
Two-year contracts bring in the most revenue (6.28M, 39.13%).
